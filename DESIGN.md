# BJBC interface foundation

Task 1 is an operational authentication surface. The specification and owner-approved English amendment govern copy and scope.

Use a quiet clubhouse visual language: deep court green, warm off-white, dark ink and a restrained lime accent. Manrope is bundled locally. Strong page titles, generous form spacing, 48px primary controls and visible keyboard focus support phone use. Court lines are precise geometric decoration, hidden from assistive technology; no invented club statistics or session records.

On desktop, a green brand panel anchors a light authentication form. On phones, identity precedes the form and the decorative panel recedes. Errors stay beside the task; pending/rejected/suspended states explain the next action. Use reduced-motion preferences; interaction state is never communicated through color alone.

Task 7 inherits this foundation for private guest session/payment pages and the
necessary invitation, verification, linking and recovery forms. Keep session facts,
explicit consent and immutable payment history easy to scan on phones. Private
links appear only in a clearly labelled manual-sharing section with Copy and Hide;
never present names or phone numbers as guest credentials.
